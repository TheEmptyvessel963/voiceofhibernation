## Who I Am

Welcome to **The Empty Vessel** — a space carved out for honest thinking, evidence-based reflection, and the kind of writing that comes from sitting with hard questions long enough to say something useful about them.

The name is borrowed from the idea that true learning requires emptying oneself of prior assumptions. It's a principle I try to live by.

## What I Write About

I explore the intersection of **science, philosophy, and everyday experience**. You'll find posts on cognition, critical thinking, the nature of knowledge, and the small observations that end up mattering most.

- 🧠 **Cognitive science** — how we think, and why we often don't
- 📖 **Philosophy** — questions worth living with
- 🔬 **Evidence & reasoning** — how to be less wrong
- ✍️ **Personal reflections** — honest writing about an examined life

## Why Write?

Writing is thinking made visible. When I write, I find out what I actually believe. I hope that some of it resonates with you too.
